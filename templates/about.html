About Me

Hi, I’m Frances! I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Dundee, studying for a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science with Industrial Placement. I enjoy building software, exploring how technology can solve practical problems, and learning how to make digital experiences more useful and accessible.

From March 2024 to February 2025, I worked as an IT & Digital Solutions Placement Student at the Department for Education in Manchester. I collaborated with developers and UX professionals on an internal web application for creating and managing accessibility statements. Alongside contributing to front-end development with HTML and Java, I took part in stakeholder interviews, requirements gathering, wireframing, prototyping and usability testing. That experience strengthened my interest in user-centred development and showed me how important it is to understand the people who will use the software.

My technical experience includes Python, Java, JavaScript, C, C++, C#, HTML and CSS, as well as working with Flask, JavaFX, SQL, MySQL, SQLite and REST APIs. I also have experience with object-oriented programming, software testing, debugging, responsive web design, Git, Unix/Linux and Bash scripting. I enjoy working across different parts of development, from planning and problem-solving to building, testing and refining a solution.

Before university, I studied a T-Level in Digital Production, Design & Development at Bury College, where I developed practical skills in software and web development, UX/UI, project planning and understanding the role of technology in business. I’m also continuing to develop professionally through the Early-Stage Career Development Program at Blackforce.

I’m always keen to learn, take on new challenges and build things that have a meaningful purpose. Whether I’m working on a project independently or collaborating with others, I aim to approach problems with curiosity, creativity and care.
