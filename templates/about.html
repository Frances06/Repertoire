About Me

Hi, I’m Frances! I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Dundee with an interest in building practical software and web applications.

My technical experience includes Python, Java, JavaScript, C, C++, C#, HTML and CSS, alongside Flask, JavaFX, SQL, REST APIs, Git, Unix/Linux and Bash. I’m also developing my knowledge of machine learning, reinforcement learning and AI agent development.

Experience beyond the code

This strengthened my communication, teamwork, problem-solving and user-centred design skills.

I’m keen to keep learning, take on challenging problems and grow as a software developer, particularly in building useful and accessible applications.
